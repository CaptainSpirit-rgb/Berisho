### 1. NVDA: down 14.0% on Friday, 22.9% over 5 days, on 2.4x normal volume
- **What happened:** On 17 Nov NVIDIA reported Q3 FY27 revenue of $115.8B (+103% y/y, +20% q/q), Data Center $107.6B (+110% y/y), GAAP gross margin 74.8% and GAAP EPS $2.70. Q4 guidance is $130.0B ±3% (the quarter has an extra week, and no China Data Center compute revenue is assumed), with gross margin guided down to 71.0–72.0%. The stock still fell 22.9% in the 5 days after. A Stocktwits headline says the SEC reportedly probes its accounting practices; that is a report, not confirmed in the filing.
- **Facts in the filing worth noticing:** Colette Kress signs as *interim* CEO, and the risk language mentions "the search for a new chief executive officer" and "the ongoing investigation by an independent committee of NVIDIA's board of directors". Receivables rose to $81.0B (DSO 64 days, from 60) partly from "extended payment terms" for some customers; inventory rose to $39.7B from $31.6B; supply commitments rose to $390B from $279B; and NVIDIA has up to $118.0B of guarantees, $105.0B of it credit support for an OpenAI-leased campus in Ohio.
- **Why it may matter for months:** The growth numbers are very strong, so the drop points to concerns about quality and risk (leadership, an internal investigation, longer payment terms, large financial guarantees) rather than demand. Those are slow-moving issues that can weigh on the stock for a long time, or clear up and stop mattering.
- **Bull case:** Revenue doubled, margins stay above 70%, guidance implies further growth, and $227.3B of buyback authorization remains.
- **Bear case:** An interim CEO, a board investigation, a reported SEC probe, and rising receivables and guarantees together raise questions about how solid the reported numbers are.
- **Check next:** Read what the independent committee is investigating (look for it in the upcoming 10-Q, which NVIDIA says it will file "in the coming weeks"), and whether the 10-Q is filed on time.
