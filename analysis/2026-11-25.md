## What matters

### 1. NVDA: down 5.9% on 24 Nov (opened 3.6% lower), flat over 5 days (-0.1%, 1.8 points better than SPY), volume 1.2x normal
- **What happened:** An 8-K says that on 22 Nov the board "reinstated Jen-Hsun Huang as NVIDIA's President, Chief Executive Officer and member of the Board of Directors, effective immediately", and Colette Kress returns to being only CFO. The press release says the board voted unanimously after it "was satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang's candor and ability to lead NVIDIA", and that it "received input from senior levels of the United States government" and is confident the reinstatement "poses no national security risk". It also says Huang had been reported as "the target of a foreign intelligence effort", the independent committee's investigation "will proceed to conclusion", a new National Security and Export Control Committee was formed, NVIDIA is searching for a Chief Compliance Officer, and it "will continue to cooperate with ongoing government investigations and inquiries."
- **Why it may matter for months:** The leadership question from last week is settled for now, but the investigations are not closed. Nothing in the data explains the 5.9% drop on 24 Nov; the 5-day figure being flat suggests the stock rose earlier in the window and gave it back that day.
- **Bull case:** The founder-CEO is back with unanimous board support, and the company is adding compliance oversight that may reduce the governance risk.
- **Bear case:** The investigation into the foreign intelligence issue, plus "ongoing government investigations and inquiries", are still open and could still produce bad news.
- **Check next:** Whether the 10-Q that NVIDIA said last week it would file "in the coming weeks" arrives on time, and what it says about the committee's investigation.

### 2. KO (you hold it): flat on 25 Nov (-0.0%), -0.5% over 5 days, 1.4 points better than SPY, volume 1.2x
- **What happened:** An 8-K (filed 23 Nov) names David Elkins, CFO of Bristol Myers Squibb since 2019, as KO's next CFO from 2 April 2027. Current CFO John Murphy, 64, "intends to retire in 2027" and stays as president in the meantime. The release says Murphy "has been working with company leadership for some time to prepare for the succession process." A Zacks headline says KO is up 6.4% since its last earnings report.
- **Why it may matter for months:** This reads as a planned, long-notice succession rather than a sudden exit, so it is low drama. The new CFO comes from pharma, not consumer goods, and will also oversee global strategy.
- **Bull case:** An orderly handover with a long overlap, and the stock keeps holding up in a falling market.
- **Bear case:** A new CFO from outside the industry can bring a change in capital allocation priorities; and the four executive vice presidents who sold $16.5M in the last 30 days (no buying) is the same selling noted last week, which is weak evidence on its own.
- **Check next:** Whether Elkins' first statements or KO's next results signal any change to dividend or buyback policy.
